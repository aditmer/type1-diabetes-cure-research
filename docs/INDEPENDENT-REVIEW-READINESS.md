# Independent clinical and statistical review readiness

It does not change Issue #59, complete independent review, establish reviewer qualifications or independence, grant source access, satisfy G2-B, authorize G2-C, or replace any formal governance requirement.

## Commissioning criteria

External review should be considered only when the following conditions are documented and can be verified:

- **Meaningful evidence coverage:** A clearly scoped research question and justified coverage of relevant human clinical evidence, registries, major harms, and negative or inconclusive results. Readiness is based on coverage and scientific coherence, not a count of extracted fields.
- **Reproducible extraction and transformation:** Frozen source versions and lawful access provenance; defined extraction instructions; stable data contract; deterministic transformation; tests and independent reproduction of outputs.
- **Cohort overlap:** All known same, subset, distinct, and uncertain relationships are documented with sources and adjudication. Unresolved overlap is visible and excluded from inappropriate aggregation.
- **Missingness and conflicts:** Outcome-level denominators, timing, planned versus observed status, unreported versus unextracted state, inaccessible content, and contradictions are explicitly reported without zero imputation or silent resolution.
- **Internal scientific review:** Primary human extraction and adjudication are complete for the proposed review scope; discrepancies and limitations have been addressed or explicitly retained. Reviewer identity, qualifications, independence, conflicts, and source-access constraints are recorded.
- **Methodology and research questions:** Methods, endpoint definitions, populations, time anchors, comparison rules, exclusions, and analysis decisions are stable, versioned, and transparent.
- **Limitations and rights:** Clinical, statistical, source-access, licensing, generalizability, and safety limitations are stated; no restricted source material is redistributed.
- **Stable review deliverable:** A versioned dataset, report, code, tests, and reproduction instructions are frozen and available to the reviewers within authorized access terms.
- **Formal governance:** Applicable reviewer qualification and independence requirements remain intact; authorized maintainer decisions and any G2-B/G2-C gates are explicitly satisfied before downstream activity that depends on them.

Failure to meet any applicable criterion means defer, narrow, or stop review commissioning and document why. Synthetic tests, code review, or internal review are not independent clinical/statistical validation.
