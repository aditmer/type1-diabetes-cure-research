## Approved prospective clarifications

| Issue in v1.0 | Approved deterministic clarification | Why needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provisional P1-P3 say "report population" and overall follow-up without explicitly binding both to the frozen target. | The quantitative clinical endpoint and overall nominal follow-up must be attributed by the report to its prespecified target, not only a contextual cohort. Simple report-stated attribution is enough for the permissive screen; verified detailed cohort/overlap mapping remains an H1 harmonized requirement. Confirmed other-cohort-only evidence fails the relevant screen predicate; uncertain attribution is unknown, not an omission. | Prevents borrowing Cohort 1, another product arm, or a responder subgroup to pass a different fixed target. Does not import all harmonized identity requirements into the screen. |
| H4 permits a stated treatment anchor but does not choose among first and repeat administrations. | Use each participant's first administration of the cell-containing intervention as time zero: first infusion or first implantation of the cell-containing product. Preparatory empty-device implantation and later repeat administrations do not reset that index. Retain all repeat exposures separately. A later-administration anchor fails; an unknown anchor remains unknown during incomplete/conflicting review, but confirmed omission after complete review fails. | The same nominal 12-month label can otherwise denote different exposure histories. This is an approved project convention, not a claim that the source's alternate anchor is scientifically invalid. |
| H2 insulin lists dose, independence definition, onset, and duration but not an explicit observed independence result. Mandatory onset/duration can also be misread as requiring success. | Require an observed insulin-independent participant count, including an explicitly reported zero, its binary analysis denominator, and the independence definition. If nobody achieved independence, onset and duration are structurally `not_applicable` with cited explicit absence. If anyone did, retain reported onset/duration. Never infer zero from a protocol or silence. | Closes the missing-observation loophole while preserving the required unfavorable-outcome negative control. This does not waive the independence outcome, definition, denominator, or horizon. |
| The prose says a field has a value "only if supported," which could be confused with report-level support. | A `reported` field retains its source-supported value, including zero, false, unfavorable results, or a fact proving a rule failure, regardless of either report label. Non-reported fields have null values and explanatory locators/notes. | A failed reporting rule must not erase the observed facts needed to explain that failure. |

For all these clarifications, v1.0 precedence remains unchanged:
confirmed no enrolled target comes first; otherwise a proven failed
requirement precedes unknown information, with all unknown flags retained.
Harmonized support still requires provisional support, and both rules still
consume the same adjudicated facts.

## Additional expected control behavior (not executed)

| Isolated hypothetical change to the original complete synthetic control | Proposed expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Put all quantitative clinical results and one-year overall follow-up in a contextual cohort only, with complete review proving none for the fixed target. | Provisional and harmonized `not_supported`; `TARGET_POPULATION_MISMATCH`, plus any other confirmed failures. |
| Make attribution of the endpoint/follow-up to the fixed target uncertain, with no independently proven failure. | Both `unresolved`; `TARGET_ATTRIBUTION_UNCERTAIN`. No inferred cohort borrowing. |
| Retain simple source-stated target attribution and overall follow-up but make independently verified outcome-to-target mapping uncertain. | Provisional remains `supported`; harmonized `unresolved` if no other failure is proven. The screen has not acquired the stricter H1 gate. Uncertain participant overlap alone still does not prevent harmonized support when target identity is established. |
| Report a required endpoint at nominal month 12 after a repeat administration only, while overall target follow-up remains at least one year. | Provisional `supported`; harmonized `not_supported` with `ANCHOR_MISMATCH`. Confirmed omission of which administration after complete review fails with `TIMING_UNREPORTED`; incomplete or conflicting anchor review gives `unresolved` absent another proven failure. |
| Explicitly report that nobody achieved insulin independence during the observation interval through the fixed horizon, with valid denominator, definition, first-treatment horizon, and all other requirements met. | Both `supported`; independence onset/duration are justified field-level `not_applicable`. A count of zero independent at month 12 alone does not establish absence of earlier independence episodes. |
| Supply an independence definition and insulin dose, but confirm no observed independence count is reported after complete review. | Provisional remains `supported` from the quantitative dose; harmonized `not_supported` with `NUMERATOR_MISSING`. Incomplete review instead gives an unknown field. |
| Keep a reported wrong-horizon observation when the harmonized label is `not_supported`. | Preserve its field value and locator; do not null it because the report failed. |
